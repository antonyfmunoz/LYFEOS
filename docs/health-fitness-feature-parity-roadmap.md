# LyfeOS Health & Fitness: Feature-Parity and Holistic-Advantage Roadmap

**Status:** canonical implementation roadmap.
**Prepared:** 2026-08-16.
**Source hierarchy:** current LyfeOS code establishes implementation truth; current official vendor documentation establishes market-baseline truth; Notion establishes current LyfeOS product doctrine; Drive supports historical intent.
**Replaces:** the execution scope of `health-fitness-roadmap.md`; that document remains useful background.
**Delivery principle:** build, qualify, and release one complete vertical slice at a time. A local implementation, a deployed migration, provider authorization, and a real-user end-to-end flow are separate evidence gates.

## 1. Decision and definition of success

LyfeOS must be at least as capable as the specialist tools a health-conscious person would otherwise combine:

| User need | Specialist baseline | LyfeOS parity definition | LyfeOS advantage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Food and nutrition | MyFitnessPal / Cronometer | Fast, accurate daily capture; complete macro and micronutrient accounting; food discovery; recipes; planning; reports | Nutrition is connected to the user's chosen goals, routines, recovery, and missions—only with explicit permission and visible evidence |
| Strength and conditioning | Hevy / Strong / TrainingPeaks-style loggers | Exercise library, fast set-level logging, templates, plans, cardio, history, PRs, progress | Physical capability appears alongside the life system without pretending that a gym log proves competence everywhere else |
| Sleep and recovery | Oura / WHOOP-style trackers | Imported or manual sleep, activity, recovery behaviors, longitudinal trends, data quality | Compare self-chosen behaviors and life context to health signals with uncertainty, never causal or diagnostic claims |
| Health record aggregation | Apple Health / Health Connect | Consent-driven source connections, provenance, duplicate handling, granular revoke/export/delete | One user-controlled private evidence layer; raw health data never becomes ecosystem-wide telemetry by default |

**Parity is not a screen count.** A capability is complete only when a user can: capture or connect it; correct or delete it; see its source and measurement; understand what is missing or uncertain; retrieve a useful trend; export it; revoke a provider; and have it behave correctly across time zones, units, retries, and authentication.

**Desired end state:** a private, user-controlled operating view of health and physical development that makes real progress visible and actionable. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, certify, or claim that an observed correlation caused an outcome.

## 3. Product and safety contract

1. **Private by default.** Health data is private to its owner. It is not sent to UMH, EntrepreneurOS, CreativesOS, a coach, an AI model, or another person unless the user selects a narrow, revocable share.
2. **User control before intelligence.** Every import, derived value, AI proposal, cross-domain contextual signal, and plan action shows inputs, source, time range, and a confirm/reject path.
3. **No medical positioning.** Show raw lab values with the laboratory-provided reference range and source. Do not interpret values, triage, recommend treatment, or imply medical clearance. Use conspicuous language directing medical questions to a qualified clinician.
4. **No body-type determinism.** “Body type” is optional self-described context. It never changes calorie, training, health, or capability calculations automatically, and it is never shown as a diagnosis or immutable category.
5. **No false causality.** An association is an exploratory, user-specific observation only. It must state coverage, sample count, time window, confounders/missingness where known, and “not proof of cause.”
6. **Source truth over score theater.** Preserve raw source records and transformations. A single readiness/health score may be an optional, explainable summary; it cannot replace source signals or trigger a high-stakes action.
7. **Gamification rewards honest process.** XP, badges, streaks, and ranks recognize verified actions, consistency, reflection, and review—not unverified claims, punitive missed-day mechanics, or health outcomes beyond the user's control.

## 5. Required data architecture

The current table set is a valuable starting point. It must evolve from domain-specific forms into a provenance-first ledger without deleting or rewriting historical user input.

| Data group | Current foundation | Required evolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and preferences | Health profile, unit/timezone/domain preferences, versioned targets, calculation/source-priority/tracking preferences, consent lifecycle and in-app cue preferences | Approved retention/notification policy and provider-specific consent versions after vendor selection |
| Measurements | Body/hydration ledgers, raw/defined observations, event-time provenance, compatible conversion registry, interval semantics and lab collection metadata | Governed encrypted attachment linkage after the media/security decision; authorized clinical import adapters |
| Food | Private foods/portions/barcodes, recipes/revisions, saved meals/plans, immutable diary nutrient snapshots and 76-key nutrient registry | Licensed catalog source/item/nutrient ingestion and territory/version attribution after vendor approval |
| Training | Exercise definitions, atomic sets, immutable workout revisions, templates/revisions, programs/sessions, cardio samples, formula-versioned analytics and evidence links | Licensed shared media/library aliases and permissioned GPS/provider bindings after their decisions |
| Recovery | Recovery activities/routines/tags, sleep sessions/naps/reflection, timeline/trends and provider-neutral normalized observations | Authorized measured-source ingestion and real-device reconciliation evidence |
| Imports | Provider-neutral connections, opaque credential references, consent scopes, cursors, leases, import runs, immutable source versions, normalized observations, suppression/dedup/canonical decisions and sanitized retry/dead-letter state | Credential-vault/live OAuth adapters, reviewed direct-vendor maps and authorized provider tests |
| Insights | Versioned deterministic trends/associations, coverage/withholding state, selected-input planning drafts, approval/action receipts and bounded optional AI requests | Independent analytic/medical review, configured provider evidence and any newly approved calculation domains |
| Progression | Health event-rule versions, append-only earning/reversal ledger, evidence references, reversible ranks/badges and private review audit | Product-language/usability acceptance and any approved new evidence rules |

### Core invariants

- Every user-owned row carries `user_id`; every access query has user scope enforced server-side.
- Preserve `occurred_at`, `recorded_at`, timezone/offset, source time, and import time. Never infer one from the other silently.
- Store canonical units plus original value/unit when importing. Conversion uses a documented, versioned metric definition.
- An imported record is immutable; correction is a new reconciliation/canonical-selection action. A manual record may be edited with an audit revision.
- Every derived total, target, trend, PR, badge, and insight references its input set and formula/rule version.
- Imported/provider tokens are encrypted or stored in the provider secret system; never place them in browser storage, analytics, logs, exports, or UMH events.
- Raw health data is excluded from generic event telemetry. Observability records counts/status/error classification, not values or notes.

## 7. Build phases, gates, and critical path

### HF-0 — Policy, provider, and architectural decisions

**Scope:** approve safety language; data classification; retention/deletion; food-data licensing; native-mobile strategy; first connector order; country/availability scope; legal/privacy review; metric and calculation registry spec.

**Exit gate:** decisions are written, ownership assigned, no forbidden data path remains ambiguous, and a testable import/consent/canonicalization contract exists. Do not add a food database, medical-record importer, or wearable OAuth flow before this gate.

### HF-1 — Native daily foundation (current partial local work)

**Scope:** profile/preferences, measurements/hydration, manual food/recipe diary, fasting/supplements, basic workout/recovery log, raw observation entry, full CRUD, private UI.

**Remaining exit criteria:** the disposable local forward/reconciliation migration, core authenticated runtime gates, owner-isolation/export/Health-deletion rehearsal, and forward-only rollback policy are complete. A managed preview/staging migration, daily phone flow, and deployment health/observability receipts remain. Production release requires separate immutable-source, migration, alert, restore, and acceptance evidence.

### HF-2 — Ledger correctness and daily logging parity

**Scope:** atomic workout sets, units/timezone, history edits/audit, saved meals/recent/favorites/copy, tags, hydration/fasting corrections, offline/idempotent capture, accessible mobile UX.

**Exit gate:** a user can reliably log an entire week without source connections; deletion/correction reverses affected totals and any derived awards; performance and accessibility budgets pass.

### HF-3 — Nutrition intelligence and catalog parity

**Scope:** licensed food catalog and barcode, serving system, nutrient registry/micronutrients, recipes/revisions, target engine, meal plan, reports/export.

**Dependencies:** food provider agreement, attribution/retention rights, catalog update strategy, no scraped data.

**Exit gate:** catalog search, scan, manual fallback, all calculations, target method/version and reports reconcile against fixtures; rights and correct attribution are verified.

### HF-4 — Training, conditioning, and capability parity

**Scope:** exercise library/custom movements, templates, programs/calendar, cardio, timers, PR/volume formulas, history, progression views, independent evidence facets.

**Exit gate:** strength and cardio user can complete, revise, plan and analyze a mesocycle; formulas are transparent and fixture-tested; no generic competence claim is generated from a workout metric.

### HF-5 — Recovery, trends, and raw health records

**Scope:** unified recovery/sleep/activity timelines, tags, metric registry/panels, governed attachments, trend workbench, user-requested associations with confidence/coverage.

**Exit gate:** charts reconcile to raw ledger; incomplete data is visibly incomplete; zero causal or clinical statements in UI/API/AI tests; retention/export/delete and attachment access are proven.

### HF-6 — Mobile health bridges and selected providers

**Scope:** native iOS HealthKit and Android Health Connect bridge first; then the smallest justified direct provider set (candidate order: Oura/WHOOP/Strava/Garmin based on approved demand and terms).

**Exit gate:** device-level grant/deny/pause/revoke, sync/backfill/cursor/retry/no-loop/dedup flows work; each provider has live sandbox or authorized test evidence, incident runbook and dashboard. Android publication requires the required Health Connect data-use declarations and least-privilege justification.

### HF-7 — Holistic advantage: insight, planning, game progression, ecosystem

**Scope:** evidence-backed comparisons, confirm-first planning actions, health-specific XP/badge ledger rules, optional scoped UMH planning context, AI proposal flows.

**Exit gate:** every output shows source/method/coverage; raw health data cannot cross an ecosystem boundary in contract tests; award reversal and consent/revoke receipts work; adverse “not enough data” paths are as polished as positive paths.

### HF-8 — Public production qualification

**Scope:** threat model, privacy review, abuse/rate limits, monitoring/alerts, Sentry privacy configuration, backup/restore drill, load/performance, accessibility, mobile store declarations where applicable, support/runbooks, staged rollout.

**Exit gate:** local qualification, CI, preview/staging acceptance, production migrations, live provider flows, live alerts and restore evidence are all recorded separately. A feature is not marked released from a green unit test or a deployed image alone.

## 8. Calculation and insight governance

1. Maintain a calculation registry: identifier, purpose, input metrics, valid units, formula, limitations, version, effective date, fixture tests, and display copy.
2. Store target provenance: user-set, imported, or calculation method. Users may change it; historic entries retain their original target/version context.
3. For training estimates (for example estimated 1RM), show only when inputs meet declared validity rules, name the formula, and avoid medical or performance guarantees.
4. For correlations, require a documented minimum number of aligned observations and coverage threshold; use explicit missing-data treatment; show direction/magnitude and uncertainty; never convert it into causal, health, or business-performance advice automatically.
5. The app may externalize progress. It cannot measure latent traits such as confidence, resilience, intelligence, or “overall wellness” as established fact without user-defined evidence and clear limits.

## 9. Integration and data-sharing contract

```text
source fact -> immutable provenance record -> normalized private health ledger
            -> user-visible canonical/source choice -> permitted calculation/trend
            -> user-selected insight -> user-approved planning context (optional)
```

- No direct database sharing with other OS products.
- No raw biometrics, food diary, lab values, notes, health attachments, or provider tokens in UMH events.
- A future outbound `health.planning_context.shared`-style event (name to be finalized under the federation contract) contains only the confirmed, purpose-limited context, consent receipt, expiry, subject/tenant identity, schema version and signature metadata.
- Incoming cross-product tasks remain a *reference* or draft in LyfeOS until the user accepts them; they do not manufacture a health log or health XP.
- All sharing is granular, off by default, revocable, auditable, and fails closed.

## 11. Delivery loop: how “loop until complete” operates

For each backlog ID, repeat this loop before taking the next dependency:

1. **Specify** the user journey, data contract, privacy classification, failure states and acceptance fixtures.
2. **Build** migration, service/API, UI and audit/telemetry without widening data sharing.
3. **Qualify locally** with typecheck, focused unit/integration tests, migration tests, ownership/authorization tests, accessibility and error-state review.
4. **Qualify as a product** in preview/staging: desktop and phone acceptance journey, timezone/unit/offline/retry paths, export/delete, source and correction behavior.
5. **Release deliberately**: review diff, commit, CI, deploy, apply migration, smoke test, observe health/error dashboards. These are separate gates.
6. **Prove external dependencies** with an authorized provider/device test: consent, minimal scopes, first sync, historical/backfill policy, duplicate prevention, pause/revoke, failure/retry, credential rotation.
7. **Close the record** only when evidence is linked to the backlog item and operational ownership/runbook exists. Otherwise the item remains in progress—not “done because code exists.”

The full desired state is complete only when every row in Section 4 is either released with all applicable evidence or explicitly re-scoped by a recorded product decision. It is not responsible to promise a date-independent “complete” label while provider contracts, native store review, and real-device acceptance remain external gates.
